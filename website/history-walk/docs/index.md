# History Walk

History Walk is a choose-your-own-path history scenario for Athenian students. Each student plays a person escaping
slavery in the nineteenth century. Every slide tells part of the story and ends in a decision; the student picks a
choice and writes down why. Some choices lead on, some end in capture or failure, and one path leads to freedom.

Teachers follow their class from two pages: a summary of where every student is, and every reason every student gave.
Before giving the walk to a class, teachers can switch the whole app to the student view and walk it as their students
will.

There are two walks, each served by its own copy of the app:

| Walk | Who you play | Where it starts |
|---|---|---|
| **Jermain** | Jarm Logue, later known as Reverend Jermain W. Longuen, on the Underground Railroad | *UGRR Scenario* |
| **Moses** | Moses, sold with his mother to a cruel slave owner, Mr. Gooch | *Introduction* |

## At a glance

- Everyone signs in with their **athenian.org Google account**. Students' addresses start with two digits
  (`27msmith@athenian.org`); teachers are the addresses an administrator lists.
- A walk is saved as it goes, so a student can stop and pick up later, on any computer, where they left off.
- A student never sees another student's walk. Only teachers see the class pages.
- A teacher's own walk is a test walk: it is left out of the class pages, unless the app is switched to the
  [student view](teachers/student-view.md).

What changed in each release, for teachers and for administrators, is in the [release notes](release-notes.md).

A question this guide doesn't seem to answer? The chat button at the bottom right of every page opens an assistant
that answers from this guide and the app's source. It can be wrong, so check its answer against the page it points
to. What you type is sent to [Context7](https://context7.com), which runs it, so leave out students' names.
