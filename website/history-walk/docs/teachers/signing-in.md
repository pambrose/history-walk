# Signing in

History Walk uses your school Google account; there is no separate password.

## Signing in

1. Open the walk's address in your browser.
2. A dialog titled **History Walk** (or a greeting your administrator chose) asks you to *Sign in with your Athenian
   Google account*. Click **Sign in with Google**.
3. Google shows its account chooser. Pick your **athenian.org** account. If it is not in the list, choose **Use another
   account** and sign in to it.
4. Google returns you to the walk, on the slide where you left off, or on the first slide if this is your first visit.

The app asks Google to show only athenian.org accounts in the chooser, and to show the chooser every time, so a
personal Gmail account signed in to the same browser does not get used by accident.

!!! warning "If Google says *Access blocked: This app's request is invalid*"

    That page, with *Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch*, means the app's sign-in settings don't match its Google
    setup. Google stops before you choose an account, so the page shows whichever account the browser has signed in,
    often a personal one; your account is not the problem. Nothing you do in the browser fixes it: tell your
    administrator (see [Google sign-in](../admins/google-sign-in.md#redirect_uri_mismatch)).

## Messages you may see

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| *That account isn't an athenian.org account. Please sign in with your school account.* | You chose a personal or another school's account. Sign in again with your athenian.org account. |
| *… isn't set up to use History Walk. Please ask your teacher if you need access.* | The account is an athenian.org account, but neither a student's (two digits first) nor on the teacher list. A teacher who sees this should ask the administrator to be added. |
| *Google hasn't verified the email address on this account.* | Use your school account. |
| *Sign-in didn't complete. Please try again.* | The sign-in was interrupted, for example by Back during sign-in, or it took too long. Try again. |

## How long a sign-in lasts

A sign-in lasts **12 hours**. After that the app asks you to sign in again; the walk is where you left it.

## Signing out

Click **logout** at the top right. Signing out ends your sign-in **in every browser** where you are signed in to
this walk, not only the one in front of you, so use it freely on a shared or classroom computer.

After you sign out, the browser's Back button shows the sign-in dialog, not the page you were on: nobody can step back
into your walk or the class pages.

!!! tip "On a shared computer"

    Sign out of History Walk when you're done, and sign out of Google too if the computer is shared. Signing out of
    History Walk does not sign you out of Google.
