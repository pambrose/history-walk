# Student reasons

The student reasons page (**student reasons** at the top right, or `/reasons`) lists every reason every student gave,
grouped by student.

## Layout

For each student, in order of their address, the page shows **User:** and their address, then a table:

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **From/To** | The decision, as *the slide they were on* -> *the slide the choice led to*. |
| **Reason** | What they wrote, exactly as typed. |

Within a student's table, the reasons are in the order the student gave them, so the table reads as the story of
their walk, failed attempts and second tries included.

## What's included

- Every decision a student explained, from the first slide on, including decisions that led to dead ends.
- **Not** included: slides with a single way forward (they ask for no reason), going back, and a choice repeated
  later (the student gives a reason the first time only).
- Teachers' reasons are left out, except those given in the [student view](student-view.md).

## Using it in class

- Compare how students reasoned at the same decision: search the page (++cmd+f++ or ++ctrl+f++) for a slide's title.
- A dead end followed by a different choice at the same slide shows a student changing their mind; their two reasons
  side by side often make a good discussion.
- Reasons are shown as plain text: whatever a student types appears as written, and nothing typed can change the
  page.

Like the summary, the page shows the reasons as they were when it loaded; refresh it to see new ones.
