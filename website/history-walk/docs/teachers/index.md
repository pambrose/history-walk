# Teacher guide

This part of the guide is for teachers using History Walk with a class. It needs no technical background.

## What your students do

Each student signs in with their school Google account and plays a person escaping slavery. Every slide tells part of
the story and ends in a decision. When a student picks a choice, the app asks them **why**, in at least five words, and
keeps their answer. Some choices lead on, some end the attempt, and one path leads to freedom. A student whose
attempt fails steps back to an earlier decision and tries another way.

Their walk is saved as they go: a student who signs in again, on any computer, is back on the slide where they
stopped.

## What you can do

| As a teacher you can | Where |
|---|---|
| Walk the scenario yourself, without your moves counting as a student's | [The walk](the-walk.md), [your teacher view](teacher-view.md) |
| See every student's progress: how many decisions they made, whether they reached freedom, and where they are now | [Class summary](class-summary.md) |
| Read every reason every student gave for every decision | [Student reasons](student-reasons.md) |
| Switch every teacher to the student view, to try the walk exactly as the class will | [Trying the walk as a student](student-view.md) |
| Clear the teachers' trial walks before the class starts | [Trying the walk as a student](student-view.md#clearing-the-teachers-walks) |

## Who counts as a teacher

You are a teacher in History Walk when your athenian.org address is on the app's list of teachers, which an
administrator keeps. Students are recognized by their address alone: it starts with two digits, such as
`27msmith@athenian.org`. Anyone else is turned away at sign-in.

If you should have the teacher view but see a student's page, or are told your account "isn't set up to use History
Walk", ask the administrator to add your address to the list (see [Teachers and students](../admins/access.md)).

## A first session, step by step

1. **Sign in** at the walk's address with your athenian.org account. See [Signing in](signing-in.md).
2. **Walk a few slides** to see what students see. Your page says *teacher* next to your name, and your decisions are
   marked *your test walk, not counted in class progress*.
3. **Open the class summary** from the *class summary* link at the top right. Before students start, it is empty.
4. **Try the walk as a student**, if you like: open *admin*, clear the teachers' walks, switch to the student view,
   and walk. Your moves then show up in the class pages like a student's. Switch back and clear again before the
   class starts. See [Trying the walk as a student](student-view.md).
5. **Give the class the address.** As students walk, refresh the class summary and the student reasons to follow
   them.

!!! tip "Two walks, two addresses"

    The Jermain and Moses walks are separate copies of the app, each with its own address, its own list of teachers,
    and its own class data. Signing in to one does not sign you in to the other, and a student's progress in one does
    not appear in the other.
