# Your teacher view

When you sign in with an address on the teacher list, your page is a student's page with a few additions. This is the
**teacher view**, the one teachers see unless the app is switched to the
[student view](student-view.md).

## What's different

At the top left
:   *teacher* appears after your name, and *(your test walk, not counted in class progress)* after your Total
    Decisions.

At the top right
:   Links students don't have:

| Link | Opens |
|---|---|
| **class summary** | Every student's progress, in a new tab titled *Class Summary*. See [Class summary](class-summary.md). |
| **student reasons** | Every reason every student gave, in a new tab titled *Student Reasons*. See [Student reasons](student-reasons.md). |
| **admin** | The switch between the teacher and student views, and the clear for teachers' walks. See [Trying the walk as a student](student-view.md). |
| **help** | This guide, in a new tab, so your walk stays open. |
| **reset** | Only if your administrator turned it on: deletes *your own* walk and sends you back to the first slide, once you confirm. Hover over it for a reminder. |
| **logout** | Signs you out, in every browser. |

The class summary and student reasons open in new tabs, so your walk stays open beside them; refresh them to see new
moves. The admin page opens in the same tab; its first link, **Back to the walk**, returns you.

## Your walk is a test walk

In the teacher view, your moves are recorded the same way a student's are (they keep your place and your bookmarks),
but the class summary and the student reasons **leave them out**. You can try every path, dead ends included, without
your moves ever appearing among the students'.

To start your own walk over:

- Use **reset**, if your administrator turned it on. It deletes your moves and reasons and sends you to the first
  slide. It affects only you.
- Or, on the admin page, **Clear teachers' trial walks**. It does the same for every teacher at once.

## Jumping to a slide

If your administrator turned on slide access, a teacher can open **`/slides`** at the walk's address: a list of every
slide by number and title. Clicking a title moves *your* walk straight to that slide, so you can look at any part of
the story without walking to it. Some slides appear in more than one place in the story; the numbers in the
*Instances* column pick one.

The jump only moves you there: it records no decision, and only your own walk moves.

## Things only teachers can open

The class summary, student reasons, admin page, and slide list are teacher-only. A student who types one of their
addresses is refused. The pages are never kept in the browser's cache or any cache along the way, so after you sign
out nobody can bring them back with Back.
